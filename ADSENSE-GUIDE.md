# 📺 SK Gen.ai — Google AdSense Setup Guide

## Step 1: Prepare Your Website ✅
Your website is already ready! Make sure:
- ✅ Website has original content (your site has 10 pages of content)
- ✅ Website is live on the internet (not localhost)
- ✅ You have a domain name (e.g., skgen.ai)
- ✅ Website has Privacy Policy & Terms pages
- ✅ Website loads properly on mobile

## Step 2: Get a Domain & Host Your Website
Before applying to AdSense, your site must be publicly accessible.

### Option A: Vercel (FREE — Recommended)
1. Go to https://vercel.com and sign up
2. Connect your GitHub account
3. Upload your website files
4. Get a free URL like: sk-gen-ai.vercel.app
5. You can also add a custom domain

### Option B: Netlify (FREE)
1. Go to https://netlify.com and sign up
2. Drag & drop your website folder
3. Get a free URL instantly

### Option C: Custom Domain + Hosting (Paid)
1. Buy domain from Namecheap/GoDaddy (~$10/year)
2. Get hosting from Hostinger/DigitalOcean
3. Upload files via FTP/cPanel

## Step 3: Add Required Pages
Google requires these pages for AdSense approval:

### Privacy Policy Page
Create a page at /privacy with:
- What data you collect
- How you use data
- Third-party services (Google AdSense)
- Cookie policy
- User rights (GDPR)

### Terms of Service Page
Create a page at /terms with:
- Usage rules
- Intellectual property
- Limitation of liability
- Account termination rules

### About Page
✅ Already included in your website

### Contact Page
✅ Already included in your website

## Step 4: Apply for Google AdSense
1. Go to https://www.google.com/adsense
2. Click "Get Started"
3. Sign in with your Google account
4. Enter your website URL
5. Select your country (Pakistan)
6. Choose payment method (bank transfer/Western Union)
7. Click "Start using AdSense"

## Step 5: Add AdSense Code to Your Website
After applying, Google gives you a code snippet like:

```html
<script async src="https://pagead2.googlesyndication.com/pagead/js/adsbygoogle.js?client=ca-pub-6708275279834644" crossorigin="anonymous"></script>
```

Add this in the <head> section of your website.

## Step 6: Wait for Approval
- Google reviews your site (takes 1-14 days)
- They check for: original content, navigation, required pages, no prohibited content
- You'll get an email when approved

## Step 7: Create Ad Units & Place Them
After approval, create ad units in AdSense dashboard and place them on your site.

## Common Rejection Reasons
❌ Insufficient content
❌ Copied/duplicate content
❌ No Privacy Policy or Terms page
❌ Site not accessible
❌ Under construction
❌ Prohibited content (adult, violence, etc.)
❌ Too many ads already
❌ Difficult navigation

## Tips for Fast Approval
✅ Have at least 15-20 pages of quality content
✅ Add Privacy Policy & Terms pages
✅ Make sure site loads fast
✅ Use a custom domain (not free subdomain)
✅ Have clear navigation
✅ No broken links
✅ Mobile-friendly design
✅ Wait 1-2 weeks after publishing before applying
